I work at the intersection of computer vision and multimodal learning — teaching machines to read the structure and meaning hidden inside images.

Dr. Tao He is a Deputy Researcher at the Laboratory of Intelligent Collaborative Computing, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). Prior to joining UESTC, he was a Research Fellow at Monash University, Australia.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Monash University in 2022, under the supervision of Associate Professor Yuan-Fang Li, with co-supervision by Professor Lianli Gao. He obtained his M.S. in Computer Science from UESTC in 2018 under Professor Jingkuan Song, and his B.S. in Computer Science from Northeast Normal University (NENU) in 2015.

His research focuses on scene graph generation, human–object interaction detection, and image retrieval.
